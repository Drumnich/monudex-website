Privacy Policy

How MonuDex handles camera, location, verification, and account data.

Last updated August 4, 2026

Overview

MonuDex is a travel collection app that verifies monument visits using an in-app photo and device location. This policy explains the information used by the website, mobile app, and related services.

Information we collect

Account and profile information

We may collect a display name, username, email address, profile details, privacy settings, and the monuments associated with your collection.

Camera, photos, and verification

When you attempt an unlock, MonuDex uses the camera to capture the photo you choose to submit. Verification records may include the image, selected monument, recognition result, confidence signals, timestamp, and review status.

Precise location

With your permission, MonuDex uses precise device coordinates during an unlock attempt to calculate distance from the selected monument. Location permission is requested when a location-dependent feature is used.

Device and service information

We may receive basic diagnostic information such as app version, device and operating-system type, crash data, language, and interaction events needed to maintain reliability and prevent abuse.

How we use information

We use information to operate the collection, verify monument visits, show progress, prevent manipulated or fraudulent unlocks, maintain safety and security, diagnose failures, provide support, and improve MonuDex.

AI-assisted image analysis

A submitted unlock image may be processed by an image-recognition provider to determine whether the expected monument is visible. The result is one signal in the verification decision. MonuDex should send only the information needed for that check and does not use unlock photos to train public AI models without separate notice and consent.

Legal bases

Where applicable, processing is based on providing the service you request, your consent for device permissions, our legitimate interests in reliability and fraud prevention, and compliance with legal obligations.

How we share information

We may share limited information with infrastructure, storage, mapping, analytics, customer-support, and image-verification providers acting on our behalf. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect users and the service, or in connection with a business transaction. We do not sell precise location data or verification photos.

Public profile or collection information is shared only according to the visibility choices available in the app.

Retention and security

Information is retained only as long as needed for the purposes above, including maintaining a collection, resolving disputes, preventing abuse, and meeting legal obligations. Retention can differ by data type. Reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures are used to protect information, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and rights

You can deny or revoke camera and location permissions through iOS Settings, although monument verification will not work without the required permissions. In-app settings may let you edit profile details, change collection visibility, remove submitted content, or request account deletion.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or receive a copy of personal information, and to withdraw consent. Requests can be sent to the contact below. Identity verification may be required.

Children

MonuDex is not directed to children under 13, or a higher minimum age where local law requires it. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children below the applicable age.

International processing

Information may be processed in countries other than your own. Where required, appropriate safeguards are used for international transfers.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as MonuDex develops. The updated date will change, and material changes will be communicated through the app, website, or another appropriate channel.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests can be sent to privacy@monudex.app.
