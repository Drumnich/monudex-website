How it works

Find it. Face it. Unlock it.

Your collection grows from real travel. Every unlock connects a place, a photo, and a moment.

  1. 01

    Get close

    Reach the verified area around the monument, roughly two kilometres from its coordinates.

  2. 02

    Take the selfie

    Frame yourself and the monument together using the in-app detector camera.

  3. 03

    Add to MonuDex

    When the image and location match, the monument becomes part of your collection.
