Three moves

Find it. Face it. Keep it.

MonuDex is deliberately simple. Your collection grows from real travel, not taps from the sofa.

  1. 01

    Get close

    Open a monument and reach its verified location zone, roughly two kilometres around the landmark.

  2. 02

    Take the selfie

    Capture yourself with the monument in the same frame. The camera sends one clear proof attempt.

  3. 03

    Unlock the memory

    When image recognition and location match, the monument joins your personal MonuDex.

Proof, not pretence

Two signals.
One honest unlock.

A monument is added only when the photo contains the right landmark and the device is inside its location range.

Visual recognitionAI compares the landmark in your photo with the monument you are visiting.

Location confirmationPrecise location confirms that the picture was taken near the real place.

Privacy by defaultYour location is used for the unlock attempt, not to create a public travel trail.
